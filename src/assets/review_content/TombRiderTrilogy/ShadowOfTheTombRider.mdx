## 3 - Shadow of the Tomb Raider

Embark on an adventure with Lara and the help of your her friend Jonah as they ventures through the tropical regions of the Americas to the legendary city of Paititi, once again battling Trinity, while racing to stop a Mayan apocalypse she inadvertently unleashed.

Shadow of the Tomb Raider is BY FAR the best looking game in the serie.
The visual quality of this game is truly astonishing, and i couldn't count the number of times i stopped to admire the beauty and immensity of some places. 

<img {...screenshots[1]} class="review-screenshot" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

The game pace is really great, the exploration side plays a bigger part than the previous entries, where it sometimes felt that fighting was permanently taking place, leaving you with no moments to take a break.

Throughout the story, you will see Lara evolve as a person and a adventurer, as she will go through a lot of different emotions.
From nostalgia, to sadness, doubts, questionning herself, her goals, and even her true purpose, but also courage.

You will see Lara change the more you progress in the story. You will enjoy a few moments of hapiness and laughter, but will also face fear, distress, questionning herself and what her true purpose/goal is.

<img {...screenshots[2]} class="review-screenshot" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

The voice acting of Camilla Luddington (Lara Croft) and Earl Baylon (Jonah) keeps getting better, and hits you deep in a few moments.
