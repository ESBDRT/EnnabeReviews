## 2 - Rise of the Tomb Raider

A year after the events on the island of Yamatai, Lara Croft is pursuing her father work and is headed to the Lost City of Kitezh to uncover an ancien mystery, on the trail of an artefact that could give immortality. But things doesn't go as planned. A ruthless organization known as Trinity is looking for the exact same thing.

Rise of the tomb raider is an upgrade in almost everything compared to it's predecessor, and is tied with Shadow of the tomb raider as my favorite game of the serie.

You will have to push through the cold and massive mountains of Siberia to find the artefact you are seeking for. 
Fighting the wilderness, but also Trinity.
Along the road you will meet certain characters, and Lara open's up a bit on her past. 
Emotionally speaking, the game is not gonna break you in pieces, but, you'll get a decent amount and variety of emotions.

Gameplay wise, the pace/amount of fights is a bit lower than the first game, and some fights can be avoided if you manage to go by the ennemies without being spotted.
Customization and weapon variety is a bit more rich, allowing you to adapt to different situations. 

The soundtrack is pretty good, but not memorable in my opinion, and the voice acting is also a bit better.

The game does carry a few of the previous entry "issues" like the invisible guns if not in your hand, and very fact paced animations, but this is far from a gamebreaking thing.

As i said, probably tied for best game in the serie with Shadow of the Tomb Raider !

On the technical side, the only issue is encountered was Lara skin sometime having no texture (turning black) for a few moments, this can be fixed by simply turning off Tessalation in the graphics tab.
