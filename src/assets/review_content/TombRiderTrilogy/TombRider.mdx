## 1 - Tomb Raider (2013) 

Explore the intense and gritty origin story of Lara Croft and her ascent from a young woman to a hardened survivor. 
Armed only with raw instincts and courage. With the help of the Endurance crew, Lara must fight to unravel the dark history of a forgotten island to escape its relentless hold.

For a game that came out in 2013, 13 years later, it is still visually good looking. However, behind those visuals is a game that feels hard to connect to in a thougtful manner.

The game starts very fast, some could say "too fast". Press start, and 1min30 seconds of cinematic later, you're arleady right in the action. 

There is barely any (if none) introduction to the Endurance crew and this is not something that builds up over time as you progress in the story, even tho some of them seem to be long time partners. The lack of lore and character building leads to the game struggling to make you feel any emotions towards anybody in the crew nor Lara and ended up being "just" an action game. The voice acting or the soundtrack also did not help to give me any particular memorable moments.

The world is composed of different biomes, with a decent range of color palettes, but it did not make me curious to really explore more.

Gameplay-wise, the game feels smooth and is fun to play, but there are some things more mentionning. You cannot sprint faster by pressing a key in this game, which is fine, could be a way to slow down the pace of the game, but a thing that contradict this is almost every movement animation like jumping and sliding being SUPER fast. This makes Lara feel light as a feather and, to some extent, almost weightless. One regrettable thing, immersion-wise, is the way weapons are handled. You're carrying several weapons, and even though Lara could visually carry all of them, as soon as you switch to one, the others simply become invisible. 

In the end, the game is fun and i enjoyed playing it, and is worth playing once if you just wanna turn off your brain and get some action.
