## <u>SYNOPSIS</u>

Like every year, the residents of Lumiere prepare themselves for the **Gommage**. A sad and early goodbye ceremony to a lot of people, caused by the Paintress, far from shore on her monolith.
Every year, she paints a number on it, and everyone this age, will vanish, forever.

Before diving any further, it's important to say that this game address the topic of greaving, and can be pretty heavy on the mind and heart.

With every gommage there is an expedition departing, composed of volunteers, mostly the next people to vanish. To this day, not a single expedition ever came back. Considered as a hopeless quest by many citizens of Lumiere, the expedition goes on the island of the monolith, with hopes to put an end to the Paintress long lasting curse, **for those who come after**. 

But to this day, no expedition has ever came back.
This year, it's expedition 33 turn, lead by **Gustave**.

With the help of a few friends, **Maelle**, **Lune** and **Sciel**, you will have to fight to uncover the dark truth about the Paintress, and everything else on that island.

The first thing i have to mention is the soundtrack, and i have good news for you.
You will have the chance, dare i say the privilege, even the blessing to have those sounds go through your ears.
This soundtrack is an absolute masterpiece, and if you are the kind of person that resonates very strongly with music, good luck, nothing will ever prepare you for this.

There is not a SINGLE TRACK that hasn't sent a shockwave of chills through my entire body. Every track fits the place and moment so well and passes on SO MUCH emotions. After booting up the game, staying in the menu for a good five to ten minutes, just listening to the music felt like a much needed ritual.

I've played god knows how many games in my life, ever since i can remember, yet, i can count on a hand the number of games that moved me this much.
One minute you are full hope, the next one you're in shambles.

During, you will travel trough many different places, and with the wide range and variety of color palettes, every place feels unique, nothing ever feels arleady seen.

The scale of some places makes you stop and stare, admiring the beauty of it.

<img {...screenshots[4]} class="review-screenshot" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

I played this game a pretty long time after it came out, because, a **turn based RPG** didn't attract me so much at first.
But, seing how people talked about it, and the number of awards this game has won. I felt like i was missing on so much.

And well, the combat system and the fact that while it's not your turn, you have to dodge or parry to counter attack, dynamize the turn based genre A LOT. 

There is a lot of ways to play, you can make a lot of different builds possible :

You want to heal a lot ?
You want to tank a lot ? You can
You want to deal a lot of damage ? You can 
You want to absolutely annihilate your ennemies ? Well, you also can, but trade the fact that you can't be healed for some more damage
And many many MANY more..

The different systems (Luminas, weapons element, passives, skill tree, and more) really allows you to play your own way and synergize between your expedition members.

And the animations being gorgeous is the cherry on top.

<img {...screenshots[6]} class="review-screenshot" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

The game comes with a really rich and complete photo mode, allowing you to frame your favorite moments forever.
From actual photograph tools, to filters (some of them are really, really cool), to custom dynamic lighting and much more, you will find everything you need so you can capture a moment in time.

The game also offers a New Game + mode for more replayability, but i will let you check that out by yourself.

On a technical side, i haven't experienced any bugs at all, but the game is pretty heavy to run.

To conclude, words cannot describe what i felt all along this playtrough, as i previously said, only a few games ever moved me that much.

Without a doubt, one of my favorite games ever, and without a doubt, one that will go down as part of the greatests ever made.
