## <u>SYNOPSIS</u>

Life is Strange is an episodic narrative adventure following Max Caulfield, an 18-year-old photography student who discovers she can rewind time, and change the past, present, and future, for the best, and the worst..

<img {...screenshots[0]} class="review-screenshot" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

I don't even know how to start, i've played hundreds of game in my entire life, every type you can imagine, but only a few ever crushed my soul and made me rethink every single part of my own life like this one did.

This game is more than just a game, it's truly an experience of self developpement, even a life lesson to some extent.

After finishing it, i immediately thought about a quote from the F&F Tokyo drift movie, said by Han, which is :
"Life's simple, you make choices and you don't look back"

But **"WHAT IF"** you could go back in time and do it ?

Im sure you've had moments in your life where you said to yourself, "What if i did things differently", or "what if i said that instead ?", Hoping that things would've turned out better, or atleast differently. 
Well, here's your chance.

<img {...screenshots[4]} class="review-screenshot" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

Every single choice you make, every action you do, everything you choose to say, will have consequences, on the past, the present, and the future. 

You're playing as Max Caulfield, an 18 years old photography student, you just came back to your hometown to study photography at Blackwell Academy, 5 years after moving away. 

Split in 5 episodes, that takes about ~2h each to complete, you will reunite with your childhood bestfriend Chloe.
Tourmented by losses, and with your time rewind powers, you will help her, seeking the truth she's been looking for all those years, as well as dealing with a few weird things happening at school. Quickly realizing that those things are bigger than what it looks like..

<img {...screenshots[1]} class="review-screenshot" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

The artistic style of the game, combined with a sweet as sugar soundtrack, truly makes the experience unforgettable. 
The game isn't trying to be a thousand things at a time, you have a single mechanic, rewinding time, but it comes with so much responsabilities.

What if that choice leads to something bad ? What would i do in that situation ? **DID I MAKE THE RIGHT CHOICE ?**
Those questions we're spinning in my head for the entirety of my playthrough, and even more after making my final choice.

The game leaves you a message, and here is my understanding of it :

Some things are just innevitable, and you can't really cheat your and others people destiny.
Bad things are meant to happen, and there is no such things as a better choice. You can make what seems like the right choice at that exact moment, and still end up regretting later for x different reasons. 
You cannot change the past, you can try to shape the future, by acting in the present, but that doesn't guaranty you will get the outcome you are hoping for.

I recommend you to read about the **[Chaos Theory](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Th%C3%A9orie_du_chaos)**
