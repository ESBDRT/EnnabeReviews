## <u>SYNOPSIS</u>

With the discovery of the **Lunum**, a rare mineral, searches and experimentations about the **Lunafilament** started.
A material capable of replicating anything, as long as they have its data.

Those research are concentrated on a lunar station, far from earth, managed by an Artificial intelligence called **IDUS**.

Communications went dark with the Lunar Facility, so the Eurotas crew is sent to inspect what's going on, but after a massive lunar quake, they end up stranded on the station.

<img {...screenshots[1]} class="review-screenshot" decoding="async" />

You're playing as **Hugh**, separated from his crew, unconcious and badly injured from the lunar quake.

You're woken up by an unfamiliar face : **Diana**, a mysterious android in the form of a young girl
She's a Pragmata, created using Lunafilament.

With her help, you will try to find your crew back, find out what is really happening on that station and go back home, on earth.

<img {...screenshots[3]} class="review-screenshot" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

What attracted me to pragmata was first the aesthetic of the game, but also the refreshing aspect of the combat system. Hugh handles the shooting and moving, but Diana is of precious help, allowing you to use different hacks on your ennemies.

From making them overheat, being stunned for a few seconds, or even go crazy and shoot their allies.

The variety and uniqueness of weapons, perks, and hacking styles is really great and allows you to customize your playstyle. 

I leaned more towards a build where Diana was dealing a LOT of damages by hacking and opening weak spots for Hugh to shoot at, at the cost of Hugh not dealing the most he could do and being pretty squishy.

And even tho Diana was the main source of damage, Hugh and her are complementary, as by hacking she opens up weak spots on the ennemies, for Hugh to deal more.

<img {...screenshots[0]} class="review-screenshot" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

I was honestly surprised by the story. I was at first just expecting some classic boom boom shoot bad guys story, but it ended up more personal, dark and emotive.

I sadly cannot elaborate much more on that, you will have to discover by yourself.

The time spent with Diana and the fact that you're alone together, really makes Hugh develop a **Father to Daughter** bond.

As you spend time by her side, you play with her, make her learn new things, and the bond created makes her become more than just an Android.

<img {...screenshots[2]} class="review-screenshot" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

The game features a collectible system that doesn't feel uselless, since everything you collect is for you to upgrade weapons, skills, or build lore.

The voice acting and soundtrack are honestly pretty good and really helps the emotions brought by the story to hit you like a truck.

Overall, a really really good surprise ! 
As said previously, i wasn't expecting to like it that much, but it's clearly a game that i will replay in the future and keep great memories of !
